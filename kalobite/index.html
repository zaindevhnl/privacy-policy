1. Who we are

KaloBite is a calorie and nutrition tracking app for Android, built and run by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data KaloBite collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you use KaloBite, you agree to the practices described here.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email and password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored as a hash, so we never see them.
  • Name, email address and Google account ID if you sign in with Google.

Profile and health information you provide

  • Sex, age or birth year, height, weight, activity level, goal (lose, maintain or gain) and target pace.
  • Your daily calorie target and protein, carbohydrate and fat split, whether calculated or set by hand.
  • App preferences such as units and whether notifications and reminders are on.

Logs and content you create

  • Food entries, custom foods, favourite and recent foods, and saved recipes.
  • Water intake, weight history and logged activities.
  • Meal scan results: meal name, estimated calories and number of items, with a timestamp.
  • Progress data such as streaks, challenges, badges, levels and scan credits.

Purchases

If you buy KaloBite PRO, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. We receive the purchase status (for example, whether PRO is active) through RevenueCat, linked to your account ID.

Technical information

Our service providers may log basic technical data when the app talks to their servers, such as IP address, device and OS type, app version, request time and error details. We use it to keep the service running and to fix problems.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone audio, or files outside the app.

3. Meal photos

When you scan a meal, the photo you take is sent securely to our server and then to Google's Gemini API, which estimates the foods, calories and macros. Then:

  • Only the result (meal name, calories, item count) is saved to your account. The photo is not stored in your KaloBite account or database.
  • To improve scan accuracy and debug failed scans, we may keep a diagnostic record of a scan, which can include the photo and the model's response, in our monitoring tool (Langfuse). Only the developer can access these records. They are kept for a limited time (see section 7) and are never published or sold.
  • Google processes the photo under its Gemini API Terms.

Please don't photograph people, documents or anything else you don't want processed. Point the camera at your food only.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the app: calculate your targets, record and show your logs, run meal scans, and sync your data across devices you sign in on.
  • To run PRO, scan credits and rewards: check your subscription status, how many scans you have left, and rewards you earn.
  • To send reminders you turn on, such as streak or logging reminders.
  • To keep KaloBite working and secure: diagnose errors, stop abuse, and improve scan accuracy.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell your personal data, we don't share it with data brokers, and we don't use your health information for advertising.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (providing the app), your explicit consent for health-related information you choose to enter, and our legitimate interests in securing and improving the service.

The free plan lets you watch a rewarded ad to earn extra meal scans. Ads served by Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google › Ads. PRO is ad-free.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera: to take meal photos for scanning. Only used when you open the scanner.
  • Notifications: to send the reminders you turn on. You can turn this off at any time in the app or in Android settings.
  • Internet: to sign in, sync your data and run meal scans.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and logs: until you delete them or your account.
  • Scan diagnostics in Langfuse: up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: as long as Google Play, RevenueCat and tax or accounting law require.
  • Technical logs kept by our providers: deleted on their standard schedules, usually within a few weeks.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account & data. This permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it from our database: profile, food, water, weight and activity logs, recipes and scan history. It cannot be undone.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account at muhammadzainulabadeen7@gmail.com with the subject “KaloBite account deletion”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California law, you may have the right to:

  • access a copy of your data, or receive it in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can edit most of it directly in the app);
  • delete your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that has already happened;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days. We will never treat you differently for using your rights.

10. Security

All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our database uses row-level security, so each account can read and write only its own records. Secret keys stay on our servers and are not built into the app. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

11. Children

KaloBite is not meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Calorie tracking is not suitable for children without guidance from a parent or health professional. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Not medical advice

Calorie and macro figures in KaloBite, including meal scan results, are estimates. KaloBite is a tracking tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional before making major changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or have a history of disordered eating.
